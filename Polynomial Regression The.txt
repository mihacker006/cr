1. What is Polynomial Regression?
Polynomial regression is a regression technique used when the relationship between input and output is curved instead of a straight line.

It creates additional features using powers of the original input.

Example:

Original feature:
\[
x
\]

Polynomial features:
\[
x,\ x^2,\ x^3
\]

This allows the model to fit curved relationships.
2. Polynomial Regression Formula
For degree 2:
\[
y=b_0+b_1x+b_2x^2
\]

For degree 3:
\[
y=b_0+b_1x+b_2x^2+b_3x^3
\]

Higher degrees create more complex curves.
3. What is Polynomial Degree?
Degree tells us the highest power of the input feature.

Examples:
- Degree 1: Straight line.
- Degree 2: Quadratic curve.
- Degree 3: Cubic curve.
- Degree 10: More complex curve.
4. What is Underfitting?
Underfitting happens when the model is too simple to understand the pattern in the data.

Example:

Using a straight line to fit data that has a curved relationship.

Characteristics:
- Training error is high.
- Testing error is high.
- Model has high bias.
5. What is Overfitting?
Overfitting happens when a model learns the training data too closely, including noise and unnecessary details.

Characteristics:
- Training error is very low.
- Testing error may be high.
- Model has high variance.
Example:

A degree 10 polynomial may fit the training data very closely but perform poorly on unseen data.
6. What is Bias?
Bias is the error caused by a model being too simple to capture the true relationship.

High bias usually leads to underfitting.
7. What is Variance?
Variance describes how much the model changes when the training data changes.

High variance means the model is sensitive to the training data.

High variance often leads to overfitting.
8. Bias-Variance Trade-Off
The bias-variance trade-off means balancing model simplicity and model complexity.
- Simple model: May underfit.
- Very complex model: May overfit.
- Balanced model: Learns the pattern and performs well on new data.

9. What is MSE in Polynomial Regression?
MSE measures the prediction error.

Your experiment compares training MSE and testing MSE for different polynomial degrees.

A good model should have:
- Low training error.
- Low testing error.
- A small difference between training and testing errors.
10. What is a Model Pipeline?
A pipeline combines feature scaling, polynomial feature generation, and regression.

Example:

Input Data
    ↓
StandardScaler
